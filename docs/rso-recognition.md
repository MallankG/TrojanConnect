## The process

**Who:** five officers (president, vice president, secretary, treasurer, programming chair); one full-time USC faculty or staff advisor, not on sabbatical or leave; at least five additional student members. One of the five officers submits. The application can't be transferred, and each student submits only one.

**New RSO:**
1. Check the [EngageSC Directory](https://engage.usc.edu/club_signup) (or [ask whether a page exists](https://forms.gle/Ai828xHoLZ1L6F539)).
2. Choose the submitting officer.
3. In the Directory, click "+Register a new Recognized Student Organization" (religious groups: "+Register a new Office of Religious and Spiritual Life"). Use a laptop or desktop.
4. Build the roster by USC email. People must have logged in once and have a visible profile.
5. Officers and the advisor accept through the mailer@campusgroups.com email. You don't have to wait for this.
6. Update group details: categories, logo, mission, goals.
7. Fill in the [2026–27 Constitution template](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/2026-27-RSO-Constitution-Template-1.pdf) as a fillable form. Don't flatten it. Include a non-discrimination statement.
8. Fill in the [Accountability Document](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/RSO-Accountability-Document-2026.pdf), after reading the [instructions](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Instructions-RSO-Accountability-Document-2026.pdf) and [example](https://campusactivities.usc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Example-RSO-Accountability-Document-2026.pdf).
9. Collect officer documents from all five officers. Each completes these between July 1, 2026 and the deadline:
   - [Hazing Prevention 101](https://usc.prevent.zone)
   - the [RSO Officer Learning Path](https://usc.reach360.com/share/learning-path/6af5ef44-a4b6-4303-8bb2-4a55d17951b5) (read ["Using This Course"](https://usc.reach360.com/share/course/f56b9b3b-59d4-42ad-adca-4aaf25ebea3b) first)
   - the [Commitment to Community](https://na3.docusign.net/Member/PowerFormSigning.aspx?PowerFormId=44d5d5cb-159a-4004-ad59-d4c62423a96b&env=na3&acct=3e63a5c5-bda2-4a75-a74a-95a6367636d6&v=2)
10. Answer the application questions: school or department, affiliations, accounts, activities with minors, travel or healthcare, typical events, elections, property.
11. Submit on EngageSC before **Nov 22, 11:59 PM**. Late applications aren't accepted.
12. Advisor requirements, due **Dec 1**. The advisor completes:
    - [RSO Advisor Training](https://usc.reach360.com/share/course/e8fe3a6b-e618-4563-ad8f-544c97fa500c)
    - the [Advisor Commitment](https://powerforms.docusign.net/3a4f015b-d3b8-4d43-adfa-5ec0f77fc227?env=na3&acct=3e63a5c5-bda2-4a75-a74a-95a6367636d6&accountId=3e63a5c5-bda2-4a75-a74a-95a6367636d6)
    - Clery Act training (2026 completion, or the [refresher](https://trojanlearn.csod.com/ui/lms-learning-details/app/course/7de66fea-10b0-46a2-ad92-a1d478452a9b))

    Then they submit through the [Advisor Requirements form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdsS-EcbWD3teNru4kaj4Leiu6bxNQDsrEPyIkkEX9e29hI-Q/viewform).
13. Review: watch email and EngageSC Chat. If the status is Requires Modifications, the submitter edits from My Activity > My Surveys/Forms.

**Renewal:** the same steps 2 and 4–13. Instead of step 1, get officer access on the group's existing page: request it with the [officer-access form](https://forms.gle/1ncsGH165vLibRWc8), or use the [re-establish form](https://forms.gle/1EjhFMiAGDzed5wq5) if the group lapsed and you aren't listed. Then open the group's page, review the blue box and update the roster. Officers and advisors retake training every year, and prior recognition doesn't guarantee renewal.

**Other paths:**
- Fall applicants don't reapply.
- Social fraternities and sororities go through Fraternity & Sorority Life.
- Club sports become an RSO first, then complete the Recreational Sports Council process.

**After a decision:** recognition runs from approval through **June 30, 2027**. An appeal is due within ten business days of the decision, by email to Campus Activities. Only recognized RSOs can operate, host events or use benefits, including creating EngageSC events (the event pipeline's EngageSC step).

**Contact:** Campus Activities, stuacts@usc.edu, 213-740-5649, SKS 410. Drop-in office hours are Tuesdays 11 AM–12 PM and Fridays 2–3 PM.

## Where USC's pages disagree

- **Members:** the process page asks for five additional members; the older FAQ says ten. TrojanConnect uses five.
- **Advisor forms:** two advisor forms are linked. TrojanConnect points to the Advisor Requirements form.
- **Recognition period:** the process page says both "August 1–June 30" and "approval through June 30, 2027".
- **Out of date:** the FAQ still mentions the 2025–26 templates and the fall cycle.

## Simulated EngageSC and the portal agent

The real EngageSC needs a USC login and has no way for other apps to connect, so for demos the backend serves a **simulated EngageSC** at `http://localhost:8000/mock-engagesc`. It's labelled as not a USC system and has no USC branding.

**Pages:**

| Page | URL |
|---|---|
| Sign in (any username and password) | `/login` |
| Directory, with the register buttons | `/directory` |
| Group page, with "Begin re-registration" | `/groups/<slug>` |
| Application form | `/register` |
| Status list | `/my-activity` |
| Simulated Campus Activities review | `/admin` |

Seeded existing groups for renewals: Demo Chess Collective, Demo Film Society, Demo Debate Union. Approved groups join the Directory.

The **portal agent** (`backend/app/workflows/rso/portal_agent.py`, Playwright with the installed Google Chrome) does what the submitting officer would do there:
- signs in and searches the Directory
- registers (new) or re-registers (renewal)
- fills in the roster, advisor and members from the tracker
- uploads placeholder PDFs for the constitution, the Accountability Document and the 15 officer documents
- answers the questions with demo text
- submits and reads the application ID

"Check status now" reads My Activity and updates the tracker. Set `RSO_PORTAL_SHOW_BROWSER=true` to watch it work. `RSO_PORTAL=off` turns the portal off, and the flow ends with the EngageSC hand-off instead.

**Demo script:** Assistant → "Start a new RSO" → fill in the checklist → "Submit our RSO on EngageSC" → **Submit through the portal** → open the **Simulated Campus Activities review** link → Approved → **Check status now** → Recognized → "Plan an event".

## How it was tested

- **Unit and API tests** (`backend/tests/test_rso.py`, 11 tests; the full backend suite is 150), including the simulated portal over HTTP and the portal agent's submit and status logic: dates and links, the readiness rules, validation (USC emails only, member minimum), that only a person can record the EngageSC status, both pathways, reminder emails, the chat routing, the API and the error envelope.
- **End to end in Chrome** (14 Nov 2026, built-in assistant, isolated test data):
  1. **New RSO:** "Start a new RSO", fill in the organization form, tick "check the directory", get the EngageSC Directory hand-off.
  2. **Roster and documents:** enter the five officers, the advisor and six members; tick all 15 officer documents and acceptances.
  3. **Remaining steps:** tick the remaining steps and the advisor's items.
  4. **Submit:** "Submit our RSO on EngageSC" shows the hand-off; record Pending Approval, then Recognized.
  5. **Hand-off to events:** "Plan an event" opens room options in the event planner. The EngageSC event answer stops pointing to recognition.
  6. **Renewal for an officer who isn't listed:** starts at "Get officer access" with the officer-access and re-establish forms.
  7. **Phone width (390 px):** layout checked.

  Then, through the simulated portal:
  - **New RSO:** the portal agent registered "Demo Robotics Society", APP-949090 Pending Approval. It was approved on the review page, "Check status now" moved the tracker to Recognized, and "Plan an event" opened room options.
  - **Renewal:** the agent re-registered the seeded "Demo Chess Collective" from its group page.
  - **Guards:** the agent refuses to submit before the checklist is ready, and won't register a group that already exists.

  The run found two bugs, both fixed and covered by tests:
  - The chat router matched "rso" inside "person", which sent event requests to the RSO agent.
  - "RSO advisor requirements" answered with the membership rules.
